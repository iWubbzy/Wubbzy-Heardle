    How to play

    Guess the Wubbzy song from a random segment in as few tries as possible.

    • Tap play to hear the clue.
    • Search for a title, then submit your guess.
    • A wrong guess or skip unlocks more of the same segment: 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, then 16 seconds.
    • You have six guesses. Everyone gets the same daily song and segment. The next puzzle begins at 00:00 UTC.

    Practise with unlimited songs without affecting your daily result.
